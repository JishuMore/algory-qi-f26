I tested eight variants and selected the best training return from four PO3 proxies before evaluating the final rule once on the test set. Selection creates a risk of fitting noise, and holding cash can miss large gains. This experiment tests a daily range-sweep-recovery idea, not the actual 10AM method: closes do not reveal intraday wicks, fair value gaps, entry fills, stops, or session targets.

I would not commit my own money based on this result alone.
